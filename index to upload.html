Why Poisoned Hearts

A welcoming studio with real artists behind it

Poisoned Hearts Tattoo is a locally owned tattoo studio on Washington Blvd in Ogden, led by owner Jovay Meshell. The City of Ogden lists us on its Art Stroll as a team of talented artists with a variety of styles, in a space that prioritizes a welcoming and comfortable environment.

We're also more than a tattoo shop. We make custom art and apparel, host paint nights, and show up around town at events like the Ogden Tattoo Convention. We call ourselves an inclusive art collective, and we mean it.

Good to know

Questions, answered

How do I book a tattoo?
Fill out the request form at the top of this page, call (801) 814-8280, or message us on Instagram. Tell us your idea, size and placement, and we'll follow up about design and scheduling. Appointments are held with a deposit.
Do you do custom designs?
Yes. All of our art is created custom, per request. Bring your idea, references or just a feeling, and we'll design something for you.
What styles do you tattoo?
Fine line, color, solid blackwork, abstract, Polynesian tribal, custom lettering, memorial pieces and sleeves. Each artist has their own strengths, so tell us what you're after and we'll match you with the right fit.
Can you cover up or rework an old tattoo?
Yes. Cover-ups, reworkings, touch-ups and finishing work are all part of what we do. Send us a photo of the piece and tell us what you'd like to change.
Do you offer piercings and permanent makeup?
We do. Piercings, including nose piercings, and permanent makeup are on our service list. Ask us about availability when you reach out.
How much will my tattoo cost?
Pricing depends on size, detail, placement and the artist's time, so we quote once we've heard your idea. Reaching out is free, and there's no pressure to book.
How do I take care of a new tattoo?
Your artist will give you aftercare instructions for your specific piece, and we're happy to answer questions as it heals. If anything looks or feels off, contact us or your doctor.
Do you host events?
Yes. Clients love our paint nights, and we show up around Ogden at events like the Ogden Tattoo Convention. Follow us on Instagram for dates.
Where are you located?
We're at 2216 Washington Blvd in Ogden, Utah 84401. Get directions or call (801) 814-8280.
